Questions

Before your first night

I’ve never played. Can I come?

Yes, please. Come for the 7:45 warm-up and the coach will walk you through the basics. Court captains mix levels so everyone gets games.

How does the queue work?

You join the line when you check in and tell us your level. Our smart match always puts the longest-waiting player on next, fills the court with players close to their level, and splits the teams so games are even. Nobody gets skipped more than twice. Winners stay for up to 2 games, but only when there’s a fair opponent waiting.

What should I bring?

Court shoes, water and a paddle if you have one. Doha nights stay warm, so hydrate between games.

Do I need to sign up in advance?

No. Just turn up, scan the QR code and type your name.

Is there a loyalty card?

Yes, the Club Card. Play 6 paid sessions, open play or DUPR, and the 7th is free.

Do you have rated (DUPR) games?

Yes. DUPR sessions are booked on Reclub. See DUPR games for how it works.

How do I contact the club?

Message us on WhatsApp at +974 3076 3431 or +974 3072 3503, or DM @dinkindonutqa on Instagram.
