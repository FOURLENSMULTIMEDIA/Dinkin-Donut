Questions

Before your first night

I’ve never played. Can I come?

Yes, please. Come for the 7:45 warm-up and the coach will walk you through the basics. Court captains mix levels so everyone gets games.

How does the queue work?

You join the line when you check in. Open courts take the next 4 players. After a game, losers go to the back of the line. Winners stay for up to 2 games, then they rotate too.

What should I bring?

Court shoes, water and a paddle if you have one. Doha nights stay warm, so hydrate between games.

Do I need to sign up in advance?

No. Just turn up, scan the QR code and type your name.

Do you have rated (DUPR) games?

Yes. DUPR sessions are booked on Reclub. See DUPR games for how it works.
