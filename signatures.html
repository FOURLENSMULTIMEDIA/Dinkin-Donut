Email signatures

Choose a person or type your details, tap Copy signature, then paste it into your email app's signature settings. It's centred, works in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, and the images load from dinkindonut.asia.

Square photos work best and stay on your device. With your own photo: download the PNG, then insert it as an image in your email signature settings and link it to dinkindonut.asia.
