Questions

Good to know.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. Watch the film above, come and see it running live at a Dinkin’ Donut open play in Doha, or message us for a guided demo on WhatsApp.

Why do you switch clubs on personally?

Every club on Courtcall is set up by a real person, so the platform stays fast and spam-free. It also means you have a direct line to us on WhatsApp from day one.

How do I pay?

Message us on WhatsApp with the plan you want. In Qatar, pay by Fawran or bank transfer. Anywhere else, pay by international bank transfer; we send the details on WhatsApp. We switch your club on as soon as payment lands. Yearly plans get 2 months free.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. Courtcall runs in any modern browser, on any phone, tablet, laptop or TV, anywhere in the world. Your players need nothing but their phone camera. We support clubs on WhatsApp in English.

Do my players need an app?

No. Everything runs in the browser on your club link. Players scan a QR, and hosts use any phone, tablet or laptop.

What if I stop paying?

Your club pauses, and nothing is deleted for 60 days. Renew on WhatsApp and you’re back on, with every player and result where you left it.
